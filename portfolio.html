ProfilePerfil

From a data-science transcript to systems running real businesses.

De un kárdex de ciencia de datos a sistemas que corren en negocios reales.

I'm in the final stretch of a B.S. in Computer Systems Engineering, Data Science specialization, at Tecnológico Nacional de México (TecNM), Zacatepec campus — a 92.17 GPA and 96% of my credits completed. Before that, I earned a Technical Degree in Information Technology from UAEM, graduating with honors.

Alongside school, I work as an independent full-stack developer, building custom applications for small businesses: point-of-sale systems, hotel reservation management, and data-analysis dashboards. My usual stack pairs React Native (Expo) on the client with Node.js/Express on the backend and AWS (Lambda, DynamoDB) on the infrastructure side.

On the data side, I've applied machine learning and NLP to real-world problems: multilingual emotion classification across 16,000 survey responses, and atmospheric dispersion modeling with satellite and emissions data. I've placed or competed in four national TecNM hackathons, plus HackMTY.

I'm looking to start my career as a Data Engineer, Data Analyst, or in a closely related role. I genuinely love this work — turning messy, multi-source data into systems and insights people can rely on — and I'm eager to keep growing in it.
